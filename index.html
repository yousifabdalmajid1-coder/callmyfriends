How to use

📝
Open the website. You can immediately play local mode by yourself or with someone on the same phone.
For online play, your friend opens the same link. One of you copies their ID and sends it on WhatsApp.
The other person pastes the ID and clicks Connect. Only one person should press Connect.
After it says connected, press Turn Mic On, then Start Call. Your friend presses Answer.
Pick Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four, or Chess. In online mode, your moves sync on both screens.
